# Medha Wellness - Daily Wellness Tracker

Medha Wellness helps you track daily wellness habits, lifestyle signals, optional wellness reminders, and personal wellness progress in one simple app.

Use the app to:
- Complete daily lifestyle check-ins
- View a general Daily Wellness Score
- Review general wellness progress summaries
- Track food preferences and wellness routines
- Explore general wellness education and lifestyle ideas
- Manage your profile, privacy, and account deletion options

Medha Wellness is designed for general wellness education and lifestyle monitoring. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, prescriptions, disease management, health clearance, or emergency guidance.

Disclaimer: This app provides general wellness and lifestyle information only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
