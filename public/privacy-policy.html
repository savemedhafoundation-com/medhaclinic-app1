Medha Wellness Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Medha Wellness provides general wellness tracking, lifestyle summaries, optional wellness reminders, profile management, product ordering, and account support. The app does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease and is not a medical device.

Data We Collect

We may collect name, email, phone number, age, gender, profile photo, delivery address, wellness check-in answers, wellness scores, diet preferences, uploaded wellness records, reminder preferences, app activity, device/auth identifiers, order details, payment metadata, support requests, and AI prompt/output metadata when AI-assisted features are enabled.

How We Use Data

We use data to provide accounts, wellness tracking, summaries, reminders, profile photo storage, delivery addresses, orders, payments, support, fraud prevention, security, analytics necessary for service reliability, and legal compliance.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data with Firebase/Google for authentication, storage, and app infrastructure; Razorpay for payments; backend hosting/database providers; OpenAI when AI-assisted generation is enabled; and delivery/support providers where needed to fulfill orders or respond to user requests.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to private records is restricted by authentication and service-side authorization. Production secrets must be stored outside the app and outside source control.

Location, Photos, and Notifications

Location is optional and user-initiated for delivery address autofill. Photos/files are optional for profile pictures or wellness record organization. Notifications are optional wellness reminders and can be disabled by the user.

AI-Assisted Content

AI-assisted content is general wellness education only. It may be inaccurate or incomplete and must not be used as medical advice. Users can report unsafe or misleading AI outputs inside the app.

Account Deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account in the app from Account Deletion. Users who cannot access the app may use the public deletion page or contact info@savemedha.com. Deletion removes the account and associated app data unless limited legal retention is required.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact info@savemedha.com.
