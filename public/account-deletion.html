Delete Your Medha Wellness Account

Signed-in users can delete their account directly inside the Medha Wellness app from Profile > Account Deletion.

Deletion removes your sign-in account, profile, wellness submissions, wellness summaries, saved addresses, and associated app data, unless a narrow legal or transaction-retention requirement applies.

If you cannot access the app, email info@savemedha.com from your registered email address or include your registered phone number. Use the subject: Medha Wellness Account Deletion Request.

We may verify ownership before deleting data.
