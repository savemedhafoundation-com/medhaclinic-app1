# Health Apps Declaration Draft

## App Category

Health & Fitness / general wellness tracking.

## App Positioning

Medha Wellness provides general wellness check-ins, lifestyle scoring, wellness summaries, diet preference support, optional reminders, profile management, wellness record organization, and product ordering. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, monitor, or manage disease and is not a medical device.

## Health Data Types

- Self-reported wellness/lifestyle answers.
- General wellness score and trend information.
- Diet preferences and routine information.
- Optional wellness record uploads.
- Optional reminder preferences.

## User Disclosures

The app displays disclaimers stating that content is general wellness education only, not medical advice, and users should consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical conditions.

## AI Use

AI-assisted content, when enabled, generates general wellness summaries or support plans from user-provided wellness context. Users can report unsafe AI output in-app. AI output is not diagnosis, treatment, cure, prescription, emergency guidance, or professional advice.

## Permissions

- Location: optional delivery address autofill only.
- Photos/files: optional profile photo or wellness record organization.
- Notifications: optional wellness reminders.
- Internet: app functionality.

## Reviewer Notes

Submit under Health & Fitness unless store listing or future functionality adds medical diagnosis, treatment, disease management, or provider-reviewed medical services.
